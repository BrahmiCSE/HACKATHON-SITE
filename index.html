Ideas are easy. Building one is the hard part.

The Echelon Young Innovators Hackathon 2026 is an inter-school hackathon for students of Classes 9 to 12, hosted by the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Echelon Institute of Technology, Faridabad.

Students form small teams, pick a problem worth solving, and build a working prototype. Teams that clear the online round come to campus for a nine-hour finale and present what they have built to industry mentors and judges.

You do not need to have done this before. You need a team, an idea, and a willingness to spend a day figuring things out.

  • Build, don't just pitch

    You leave with a prototype that runs, not a slide deck about one.

  • Mentors on the floor

    Industry mentors are there through the finale to unblock you.

  • Judged on the work

    Present to a panel of judges at the end of the day.

How the hackathon runs

  1. 12 October to 30 October 2026

    Registration

    Form your team and register. Open to students of Classes 9 to 12.

  2. 31 October to 5 November 2026

    Online round

    Submit your idea online. Shortlisted teams go through to the finale.

  3. 10 November 2026

    Results of the online round

    Shortlisted teams are announced and invited to campus.

  4. 21 November 2026, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM

    Offline grand finale

    Nine hours of building at Echelon Institute of Technology, Faridabad, followed by presentations to the judges.

Who can take part

Classes 9 to 12

Only school students from Class 9 to Class 12 are eligible to take part.

Teams of 2 to 4

You cannot enter alone. Minimum two members, maximum four.

One school per team

Every member of a team must be from the same school.

Online, then on campus

The first round is online. Shortlisted teams attend the finale in person.

Questions

Who can participate?

Students of Classes 9 to 12 only, in teams of two to four. All members of a team must be from the same school.

When and where is the finale?

21 November 2026, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, at Echelon Institute of Technology, Faridabad.

Is the whole event in person?

No. The first round runs online from 31 October to 5 November 2026. Only shortlisted teams attend the finale on campus.

Can I take part on my own?

No. The minimum team size is two.

Do I need to know how to code already?

Some coding ability in the team helps, but the hackathon is aimed at Class 9 to 12 students and beginners are welcome.

What are the themes or problem statements?

To be announced before the online round opens.

How do we register?

Use any Register button on this page. It opens the official listing on Unstop, where one member signs up and adds the rest of the team. Registration is open from 12 October to 30 October 2026.
